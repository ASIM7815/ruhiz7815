================================================================

The login page is trying to use Google authentication, but Supabase doesn't have it configured yet.

--------------------------------------
1. Open: https://supabase.com/dashboard/project/ybmauetbeakurugikmpb/auth/providers
2. Scroll down to find "Google" in the list of providers
3. Click on "Google" to expand it

-------------------------------
1. Toggle the "Enable Google provider" switch to ON
2. You'll see fields for Client ID and Client Secret

STEP 5: Save Configuration

Click "Save" at the bottom of the Google provider settings.

STEP 6: Update Google Cloud Console
------------------------------------

4. Should now work!
